    Before you begin.

    Practical answers about the planner, artwork and stand preview.

    Back to the planner →
    Where can I place a campaign?

    The map currently demonstrates locations in London, Birmingham and Manchester. Include or exclude cities, then explore the example venues. Venue names, rates, audiences and footfall are illustrative and do not represent confirmed inventory.

    What artwork can I upload?

    Use a JPG, PNG or WebP image up to 10 MB. Portrait A5 artwork (148 × 210 mm) fits the central leaflet pockets. The preview preserves your image proportions and adds white space where needed rather than cropping. Print-ready files would need a separate production check.

    How is the estimated price calculated?

    The estimate adds the weekly rate of every selected position and multiplies it by your chosen number of weeks. A venue match checks that venue on its own; the campaign total checks all your selected positions together. Print, delivery and other charges are not included or quoted in this prototype.

    Does a leaflet placement include the screen advert?

    The animated screen shows how the stand concept can look in a venue. Screen airtime is not part of the leaflet placement estimate. Any digital advertising offer, timings and specifications would need to be agreed separately.

    Can I book or pay through this page?

    This version creates an illustrative campaign plan and order preview. It does not take payments, reserve placements or submit booking requests. Confirmed venue inventory and a booking process would be needed before a campaign can go live.
