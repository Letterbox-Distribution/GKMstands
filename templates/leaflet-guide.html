Before you send your artwork

  • Check the size

    Set the design to A5 portrait, 148 × 210 mm. Ask us to confirm final file and bleed requirements.

  • Print a proof

    At actual size, check the headline, offer and terms. Keep important text away from the trim edge.

  • Try the next step

    Scan the printed QR code on a phone. Include a short web address and check the offer page on mobile.
