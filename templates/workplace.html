Before you book

What does the placement fee cover?

Your estimate covers the selected A5 positions and weeks. The final quote confirms placement and sets out any charges for design, production, delivery, replenishment or placement photography.

Can I request a particular office or sector?

Yes. Tell us which industries, employers or areas you want to reach. We’ll confirm suitable buildings and available positions when we review your brief.

Can I send the artwork later?

Yes. You can request a placement while artwork is in progress. We’ll confirm your artwork deadline and production schedule before booking.
