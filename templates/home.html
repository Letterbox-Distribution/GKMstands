    A few useful details

    Before you begin.

    Need a hand? Talk to Gold Key Media.

    What am I booking?

    Printed A5 leaflet placement in your chosen stand positions and venues, for an agreed number of weeks. Your request is reviewed by our team before anything is booked.

    Can I choose my position on the stand?

    Yes. Each stand has six central A5 positions: two on the top shelf, two in the middle and two at the bottom. The numbered layout shows the position and weekly rate before you add it.

    Does my artwork need to be ready?

    No. You can request your placement first and provide artwork later. To preview your design, upload an A5 portrait JPG, PNG or WebP up to 10 MB. We’ll confirm final print requirements with you.

    How are printing and delivery arranged?

    We’ll agree print quantities, production requirements, delivery and replenishment arrangements with you before your campaign is booked. Your final quote will set out what is included.

    What happens after I send a request?

    You’ll see a request reference and a summary of your selections. Our team will review your preferred venues, positions and dates, then contact you using the details you provide. No payment is taken on this website.
